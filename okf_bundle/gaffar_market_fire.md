# Gaffar Market Fire — November 7, 2026

A fire broke out at a shop selling mobile phone batteries in a four-storey commercial building at Puri Plaza in Chain Wali Gali, Gaffar Market. The blaze gutted about 40 shops.

The deployment was increased to 25 fire tenders as the blaze intensified. The fire took nearly 12 hours to be brought under control, with operations continuing till 1 pm.

DCP (Central) Rohit Rajbir Singh said the fire could have been triggered by overheating or over-charging of phone batteries.
