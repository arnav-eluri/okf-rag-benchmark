# Chawri Bazar Building Collapse — November 7, 2026

Five labourers were working at a collapsed four-storey building in Chawri Bazar on Thursday morning: Kishori Sah, Sanjeet, Laxman Mukhiya, Vipin Ram, and Shantun.

Kishori Sah's family lived in Khagaria district in Bihar. The labourers were migrant workers from Bihar.
