FlyDubai flight FZ 1073 from Dubai to Tel Aviv was involved in a serious incident where the captain stabbed the co-pilot in an apparent attempt to crash the aircraft. The co-pilot, Captain Smit Machchhar, an Indian national, sustained serious injuries. The aircraft descended from 33,000 feet to 17,000 feet in about a minute before other crew members intervened. The flight was carrying 174 people, including dozens of Israelis, and made an emergency landing in Tabuk, Saudi Arabia. The captain was overpowered and the aircraft landed safely.
