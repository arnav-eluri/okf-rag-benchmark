# Delhi GRAP III — November 7, 2026

GRAP III kicked into action Friday as a precautionary measure, with the city's air quality index steadily moving towards 'severe' (AQI above 400). Average AQI was 382 at 4pm, against 369 the previous day, nudging CAQM to further GRAP.

The move comes four days after the second stage of the graded pollution action plan was imposed.

GRAP III includes a ban on major dust-generating construction and demolition work, closure of stone crushers and mining, restrictions on BS-III petrol and BS-IV diesel cars in Delhi and five high-vehicle-density NCR districts, possible hybrid classes up to Class V, and 50% WFH in offices.

The most polluted areas were Punjabi Bagh, with an AQI of 455, DTU 455, Jahangirpuri 449, Rohini 449, RK Puram 443, Mundka 438, North Campus 431, Najafgarh 430, and Vivek Vihar 430.

Supreme Court noted that enormous judicial time spent over years on Delhi pollution had yielded only marginal benefits, and said that when more and more restrictions, without scientific basis, were put in place, people tended to violate them with a vengeance.
