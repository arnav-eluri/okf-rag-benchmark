The Centre has tightened norms for passenger vehicles through the CAFE-III (Corporate Average Fuel Economy) notification. The new CAFE-III regulations set stricter fuel efficiency and emissions standards for automakers, requiring passenger vehicle manufacturers to meet higher average fuel economy targets across their fleet. These norms are part of India's push to reduce vehicular emissions and improve energy efficiency in the automotive sector.
