# ED Investigation into $1 Million Foreign Transfer — November 7, 2026

The Enforcement Directorate (ED) has initiated an investigation against two foreign entities for their alleged involvement in India's domestic affairs and in connection with a suspicious transfer of $1 million to a UK-based firm having links to India.

Dr Matthias Karl Vogt Trust of Germany has been identified as the actual source of the $1 million fund, which was transferred to a UK-based entity through layered transactions before being moved to India.

The German trust had first transferred the funds to XBase Digital UAB (Lithuania) to get around German law. Digital assets were suspected to have come from 'hawala' (illegal money transfers) to Dubai and to the Lithuanian entity, before they were moved to the UK.

ED has identified TCC Digital Markets Ltd, a firm registered in the tax haven of Saint Kitts and Nevis, as the source of funds that reached entities and individuals in India through layered transactions. The fund finally took the form of a "grant" transferred in two tranches of GBP 3,67,006 and GBP 3,71,746 to the UK firm, before they reached end users in India.

The enforcement agency has also come across a transaction by a Canadian-registered entity, ZBase Digital Inc, which had transferred $500,000 in February to the same UK-based firm.
