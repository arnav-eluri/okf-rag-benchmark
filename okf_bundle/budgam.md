Security forces killed a Lashkar-e-Taiba (LeT) commander in Budgam, Jammu and Kashmir. The militant was active in the Pir Panjal Valley and south Kashmir region. Lashkar-e-Taiba is a Pakistan-based militant organization designated as a terrorist group.
